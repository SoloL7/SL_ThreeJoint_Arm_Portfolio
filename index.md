Hi, my name is Solomon Lloyd, I am in the 11th grade attending Friends Seminary. My choice of project from BlueStamp was the robotic arm. The functions of my project all the arm’s base to turn 360. The actual arm can move forwards and backwards, and the claw can open and close allowing for picking up objects. The arm works on a four servo system, with the first servo serving as the turner for the base plate. The second servo is for larger movements of the arm up and down while the third servo on the smaller part of the arm is for more precise movements.
